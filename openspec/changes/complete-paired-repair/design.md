This plan executes Type (1): make the pair perform its already-specified jobs.
Theory defines the observable and what may be inferred. The performance-engineering
document defines the work method: identify the question, the information needed for
the decision, the producer/consumer path and the earliest cheap operation that can
answer it. Missing premises remain named unknowns; they are not implementation choices.

Read the applicable complete sections of ARCHITECTURE (especially §11),

## Evidence flow and implementation protocol

For each task, record the specific question, admissible input/context, protected
obligation, named outcomes, producer and every consumer. Reproduce current behavior
through the real entry point before editing. Locate sibling paths and actual callers;
extract an expressible pure decision only when it clarifies a real semantic boundary.
Use intent tests for impure instrumentation and durability boundaries.

Preserve independent ledgers: behavioral disposition, measurement validity and resource
payoff. A rejected observation is not a target crash; a wall timeout is not a kill;
a stated step bound is not a proof of infinite execution. A paired performance claim
requires the same accepted question/basis, not merely a shorter test run.

An unknown engine refusal carries diagnostic counts without a certified bound or
verdict. It never prescribes an unsupported rerun. Known shape hazards are conservative
shape evidence with provenance, affected TestIds and execution mode; `--isolated`
requests a different measurement boundary, not guaranteed certification. Absent,
explicitly unknown, contained and uncontained evidence stay distinct.

## Cleanup and optimization restrictions

Keep empirical mutant groups presentation-only. W#41's original TCE-key reuse stands
separately from W#40/#42's unfinished module-scan/allocation savings. Size-only cache
checks do not prove invalidation under same-size rebinding or module replacement.

Coverage/line overlap is only a candidate-discovery signal. Removal must preserve
exact identities, assertions/error-path expectations, operator and MUST-NOT witnesses,
lines/arcs, codomain, fixtures/helpers/imports, residual standing and cross-repo
consumers. Stage the exact edit in a disposable copy, verify fresh, reject intervening
edits and commit source/certificate/index atomically. Verify combined removals jointly.
There is no deletion quota; unknown or unobservable obligations require retention.

## Deferred additions and owner premises

proof-grade static subsumption in W#25 deferred. Mixed issues retain their Type (1)

Do not manufacture the code-kappa graph/domain, the broad-handler narrowing target,
structural/effect operator premises, unmeasured step-cap parameters or a weaker meaning
of fresh reproducibility. Record the missing premise and continue independent tasks.

## Completion and recovery

A checked task needs current source/commit basis, its accepted obligations, the full
receipt or stable public CI link, and explicit limits. A suite pass is not an issue
closure. Preserve raw refusals and before images. Failed recovery stops later cleanup.
After each accepted Wesker push, refresh Detective's exact lock and sync; wheels retain
plain package requirements. Releases follow owner authorization and publish Wesker first.
