Almost everything else the theory states as new has an independent prior or parallel formalization it must now cite and reconcile — and the gap-fill supplies the sharpest of these. THE COMPUTABILITY/TRACTABILITY WALL is now fully instrumented and is the single most important correction: σ's kernel is, verbatim, three named classical objects — the equivalent-mutant problem (Budd-Angluin: UNDECIDABLE, so σ is not a total recursive function and the Blum-measure claim must be relativized to a decidable μ / an equivalence oracle), Minimum Set Cover / Test Cover (Feige + Dinur-Steurer: NP-hard and inapproximable below (1-o(1))·ln n; Chakraborty-Foucaud ISAAC 2024: resists even kernelization double-exponentially in the parameter), and Morris contextual equivalence (the unnamed relation σ 'bounds'). So 'σ = the minimum suite' is a well-defined LOWER BOUND, not a computed object; consolidation-to-minimal-witness is greedy BY NECESSITY (forced by inapproximability), not by design, and its worst-case ratio is the known Θ(log n) set-cover gap, not a novel compression guarantee. CHANNEL ISOLATION is now the most heavily contradicted claim, from four independent directions the earlier map only half-saw: (i) in the standard ILP learner (Popper LFF) the two signs are DUAL directions on ONE generality/subsumption lattice — positive failure generalises up, negative failure specialises down — not orthogonal channels; (ii) De Nicola-Hennessy may/must testing is the 40-year-old formalization of exactly the two-sign object, and its may/must preorders are provably INCOMPARABLE — which disciplines orthogonality into a theorem-with-hypotheses rather than a free assertion; (iii) Liskov-Wing behavioral subtyping treats the codomain restriction as ONE postcondition predicate whose refinement is coupled to the implementation via strengthen-post/weaken-pre; (iv) Kushigian et al. Orthogonality must weaken to 'non-redundant / positive-conditional information,' be scoped to context-reachable distinctions, and be argued at the state-field level, not the return-value level. THE FINITE-WITNESS PREMISE gains a hard new bound: Alpern-Schneider safety/liveness proves only safety/co-safety properties have finite bad prefixes, so any μ⁻ perturbation manifesting as a liveness violation (eventually-returns, terminates) is un-monitorable and cannot be value-killed by any finite suite; the value-kill/run-kill/survivor trichotomy is term-for-term the LTL3 ⊥/⊤/? verdict (Bauer-Leucker-Schallhart) with impartiality/anticipation, and 'pseudo-tested' has an exact algorithmic twin in VACUITY detection. ### Threats (full text)

**T1** (critical) — (1) σ = MINIMUM suite killing every NON-equivalent mutant; the 'σ is a Blum complexity measure' proof
- cite: Budd & Angluin, 'Two Notions of Correctness and Their Relation to Testing' (Acta Informatica 18(1), 1982); Ammann-Delamaro-Offutt ICST 2014 §I & fn.2; Offutt-Pan STVR 1997
- change: Deciding mutant equivalence reduces to program equivalence and is UNDECIDABLE, so the predicate 'non-equivalent mutant' is not recursive and 'mutation-complete' is not a total recursive functional. Blum's axioms require the measure's graph to be recursive, so the intrinsic (all-tests) σ is exactly the undecidable true-minimal-set. State explicitly whether σ is the undecidable intrinsic quantity or a computable test-set-relative estimate σ̂ (Ammann: 'we replace the risk of equivalent mutants with the risk of incomplete test sets'); relativize the Blum-measure claim to a decidable mutation policy μ or an equivalence oracle. Read 'minimum' throughout as 'minimum modulo an undecidable equivalence.'

**T2** (critical) — (4) consolidation to the minimal σ-witness = an efficient sample-compression scheme; (3) σ-dynamic = RTD as a tractable greedy trajectory
- cite: Chakraborty, Foucaud, Majumdar, Tale 'Tight (Double) Exponential Bounds… Test Cover' (ISAAC 2024, LIPIcs 322:19); Feige (JACM 1998) + Dinur-Steurer (STOC 2014); Ammann-Delamaro-Offutt ICST 2014 fn.3; Moret-Shapiro (SIAM JSSC 1985)
- change: σ(P,μ) IS the Test Cover / Minimum Set Cover problem (the minimum family separating all non-equivalent mutants). Even given the finite kill matrix it is NP-hard AND inapproximable below (1-o(1))·ln n unless P=NP, and Test Cover resists kernelization double-exponentially in the parameter k. So the minimal σ-witness is a well-defined LOWER BOUND, not a poly-time / poly-kernel object; the greedy consolidation trajectory is FORCED by inapproximability, not chosen, and its worst-case ratio is the known Θ(log n) set-cover gap. Restrict any tractability claim to bounded-arity μ (Crowston et al.'s bounded-test-size regime) and state the ln n / double-exponential barrier as a hardness result, not an engineering convenience.

**T3** (high) — (4) consolidation = sample-compression, applied to the two-sign object σ(P, μ∪μ⁻)
- cite: Pabbaraju 'Multiclass Learnability Does Not Imply Sample Compression' (ALT 2023/24, arXiv:2308.06424); repair: Hanneke-Moran-Waknine 'List Sample Compression' (COLT 2024, arXiv:2403.10889)
- change: The two-sign object carries TWO labels (positive=which-implementation, negative=must-not), so it is genuinely multiclass; dimension-bounded sample compression is PROVABLY unavailable for a multiclass class from any function of its dimension alone. The clean binary VCD→compression law does not transfer to the headline object. Recast σ(P,μ∪μ⁻) as a LIST sample-compression scheme — the population-derived censors and negative channel are set-valued/forbidden-SET outputs, exactly the regime where compression↔learnability is restored. The theory's own censor structure is the fix for its own threat.

**T4** (high) — (2) channel isolation / information-theoretic ORTHOGONALITY of the positive and negative channels
- cite: Cropper & Morel 'Learning programs by learning from failures / Popper' (Machine Learning 110, 2021); De Nicola & Hennessy 'Testing Equivalences for Processes' (TCS 34, 1984); Liskov & Wing 'A Behavioral Notion of Subtyping' (TOPLAS 16(6), 1994); Kushigian et al. 'Equivalent Mutants in the Wild' (ISSTA 2024); Papadakis et al. TCE (ICSE 2015)
- change: Four independent, web-verified counter-structures converge: (i) Popper — in the standard ILP learner the two signs are DUAL monotone directions on ONE subsumption/generality lattice (positive failure generalises up, negative failure specialises down), not orthogonal channels; (ii) De Nicola-Hennessy — the two-sign object is may/must testing, whose preorders are provably INCOMPARABLE (orthogonality is a theorem WITH hypotheses, not a free assertion); (iii) Liskov-Wing — the codomain restriction is ONE postcondition predicate coupled to implementation identity by strengthen-post/weaken-pre under refinement; (iv) Kushigian ISSTA 2024 (ground truth) — stub/extreme mutations, i.e. μ⁻'s universal family (return None/zero/constant), are a LEADING source of value-EQUIVALENT mutants, and TCE shows such value-equivalent constructs frequently compile byte-identical (zero distinguishable information). Weaken 'orthogonal' to 'non-redundant / positive-CONDITIONAL information,' scope it to context-reachable distinctions (exclude contextual-equivalent constructs, GEM-LLM 2026), and argue it at the state-field-observability level (State Field Coverage, ASE 2025), not the return-value level.

**T5** (high) — (1) value-kill / finite witness for μ⁻; (4) consolidation-to-a-finite-minimal-witness
- cite: Alpern & Schneider 'Recognizing Safety and Liveness' (Distributed Computing 2(3), 1987; IPL 1985); Aceto-Achilleos-Francalanza-Ingólfsdóttir-Lehtinen 'An Operational Guide to Monitorability' (SoSyM 20(2), 2021); Pnueli & Zaks (FM 2006)
- change: Every trace property = safety ∩ liveness; only SAFETY/co-safety properties have finite bad/good prefixes, and only the safety∪co-safety fragment is monitorable. Any μ⁻ perturbation that manifests only as a liveness violation (eventually-returns-non-None, eventually-terminates) has NO finite bad prefix and is un-monitorable, so it cannot be value-killed by any finite suite. Adopt bad/informative-prefix as the witness primitive, and gate the finite-killability of each μ⁻ operator on a per-operator monitorability check rather than assuming it. Even within safety, Kupferman-Vardi's pathologically-safe class lacks a checkable informative prefix — a caveat to inherit.

**T6** (high) — (6) benchmark/curriculum duality; the implicit 'σ measures real quality' premise
- cite: Papadakis, Shin, Yoo, Bae 'Are Mutation Scores Correlated with Real Fault Detection?' (ICSE 2018); Just et al. FSE 2014 (coupling)
- change: Once suite SIZE and subsuming mutants are controlled, mutation-score↔real-fault correlation weakens to near-zero (the correlation was a size confound). Explicitly DECOUPLE σ from real-fault prediction: σ formalizes teaching/query complexity of the μ-INDUCED semantic-equivalence class (size-invariant by construction), robust to how the coupling debate resolves. Any empirical σ-benchmark must control suite size or it re-derives the confound.

**T7** (high) — (4) consolidation / minimal σ-witness; (1) σ as a comparative measure

- change: Using subsuming AND subsumed mutants together produces Type-I errors ~62% of the time; only the disjoint/subsuming (dominator) set is a sound unit. Any σ-witness computed against a raw mutant population inherits this inflation, so consolidation to the minimal witness is a SOUNDNESS PREREQUISITE, not an optimization, and value-kill vs run-kill must be measured on the consolidated set. Define σ against a subsumption-reduced, equivalence-quotiented mutant space as a stated precondition. (Dual-edged: also corroborates 'the proof suite is the proof, not the count.')

**T8** (medium) — (3) σ-dynamic = Recursive Teaching Dimension = greedy specification trajectory

- how: Theorem 2 proves every minimal mutant set has the SAME cardinality — the exact well-definedness result the teaching-dimension/consolidation claim needs; cite it as the invariant underwriting 'σ is a dimension.' **R2** — Detective's symbolic-default + dynamic-witness architecture; the intrinsic-vs-computable σ distinction

**R3** — (1) value-kill vs run-kill = checked coverage; the two-sign split
- cite: De Nicola & Hennessy 'Testing Equivalences for Processes' (TCS 34, 1984); Bauer-Leucker-Schallhart 'Runtime Verification for LTL and TLTL' (TOSEM 20(4), 2011)
- how: may-testing (some context succeeds = positive/existence) and must-testing (every context succeeds / a refusal is observable = negative/MUST-NOT) are the canonical two-polarity test semantics the two-sign object rediscovers. The value-kill / run-kill / surviving-mutant trichotomy is term-for-term the LTL3 ⊥/⊤/? verdict, with impartiality (no final verdict while a continuation could flip it) and anticipation (commit at the first good/bad prefix) as proven properties. **R4** — (1) value-kill vs run-kill = checked coverage

**R6** — (1) σ 'bounds behavioral identity'; the μ-relativity of σ

**R7** — (2) the negative channel carries information the positive cannot; (5) κ-scored censors

The identifying-codes literature already proves the log-n density PRICE of this unique-labeling requirement, giving a quantitative handle on the negative channel's extra discriminative cost that claim (2) currently asserts without a number. 'Discriminating codes' (bipartite tests-vs-objects) is an even closer structural match to suite-vs-implementation separation.

Prior art for κ=graph-centrality test selection already exists as REDUNET (Applied Network Science 5, 2020) — position κ-PageRank against it rather than as novel.
